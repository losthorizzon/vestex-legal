Privacy Policy

This privacy policy ("policy") explains how Vestex ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with your use of the Vestex mobile application ("app"). We reserve the right to change this policy at any time; please check back periodically for updates.

What Information We Collect

Vestex does not require an account or sign-in, and does not collect any personal information. We do not collect your IP address, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your simulated cash balance and stock holdings
  • Your trade history
  • Your watchlist and price alerts

This data is never sent to us. We operate no servers in connection with the app.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple provides your display name and avatar so the app can show a leaderboard. Game Center is operated by Apple Inc. and governed by Apple's own privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple ID or Game Center account details.

Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security features. You are responsible for keeping your device secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to the support contact listed on our App Store page.
