Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · Vestex for iPhone

In short

  • No developer servers. The developer collects no personal data, identifiers, location, contacts or analytics, and there are no ads or trackers.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional. Vestex asks only for your name and keeps it, with your Apple user identifier, on your device.
  • Your iCloud backup is private. When signed in, a sealed copy of your portfolio is stored in your own private CloudKit database. The developer cannot read it.
  • Game Center is optional and run by Apple. Your alias, percentage return and rating tier are visible to other players on the leaderboard.
  • One time check. The app reads the date from a request to apple.com (or google.com if Apple can't be reached) to keep a trusted clock. No personal data is sent.
  • You can erase everything with Settings > Delete Account & Data, including the iCloud copy.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how Vestex (the "Application") handles information. The Application is an educational simulation of securities trading. It does not execute real transactions and does not hold, transfer or manage real money. Results are hypothetical.

By using the Application you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, you should stop using the Application.

2. Information We Collect

The developer operates no servers and does not collect, receive or have access to your personal information. Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, the Application receives your name and an Apple user identifier from Apple (it does not request your email address) and keeps them on your device. The Application contains no registration form.

The Application contains no third-party advertising or analytics software and does not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, photographs or usage analytics.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Your simulated portfolio, orders, transaction history, watchlist, price alerts, achievements, lesson progress, season records, practice-mode state and preferences are stored on your device in the Application's local storage.

To protect the integrity of the simulation, the Application also stores in your device's Keychain a backup copy of the portfolio, a randomly generated key used to sign saved data, and clock data (the last time confirmed by Apple's servers and the latest time observed). Keychain items stay on this device, are not synced, and contain no personal information.

To measure elapsed time, the Application reads the device's system boot time. It is used only on your device and is not transmitted.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show a small snapshot of your simulated portfolio and watchlist. The snapshot is saved in an App Group container that only the Application and its widgets on your device can read.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. Apart from the optional iCloud backup and Game Center described below, it does not leave your device.

4. Sign in with Apple and iCloud Backup

Sign in with Apple is optional. When you use it, Apple provides the Application with your name and a user identifier. The Application stores them on your device (in local storage and the Keychain) to show your name and to recognise your account. It does not request your email address. Sign in with Apple is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

When you are signed in, the Application backs up your saved portfolio data to your iCloud account. The backup is a sealed (encrypted) copy stored in your own private CloudKit database, which only you can access with your Apple ID. The developer cannot read it. It is stored and processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Delete Account & Data also removes this iCloud copy.

5. Game Center

The Application uses Apple Game Center for a leaderboard. Game Center is optional. When you are signed in to Game Center, the Application submits the percentage return of your simulated portfolio since it started or was last reset as a score, together with your rating tier in the score's context, and shows your Game Center alias and photo. Your alias, score and rating tier are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Practice-mode results are never submitted.

The Application allows one portfolio per Game Center player. If you use Delete Account & Data and then start a new portfolio, the new portfolio's scores are held back from the global leaderboard until the 6-month reset date of your previous portfolio. To apply this rule, the Application stores your portfolio's start date and your rating in the Game Center score's context, together with the score.

The leaderboard also shows five computer-run AI traders. They are simulated, labelled as AI, generated on your device, are not real persons, and involve no personal information.

Game Center is provided and operated by Apple Inc. and is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your Apple ID or any other Game Center account information. Scores already submitted are retained by Apple and are not removed by Delete Account & Data.

6. Trade Coach

The Trade Coach reviews your simulated trades using rules that run on your device. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the Application may use Apple's on-device language model to rephrase its tips. This processing takes place on your device; no trade data or tip text is sent to the developer or to any third party. You can turn the Trade Coach off in Settings.

7. Sharing

The Application can create share cards (images of simulated results) and an App Store link. They are created on your device and shared only when you choose to share them, through the iOS share sheet, with the app and recipient you select. The developer does not receive them. Once shared, they are subject to the policies of the recipient or service you choose.

8. Notifications

If you create a price alert, place a limit or stop order, or turn on a reminder, the Application asks for permission to display notifications. Notifications, including order alerts, market reminders and streak reminders, are scheduled locally on your device and involve no remote server. You can turn each kind off in the Application's settings or withdraw permission at any time in your device's Settings.

9. Market Data

Prices shown in the Application replay historical market data as if it were happening now. The data and the company logos are bundled with the Application, and prices are calculated on your device. They are not live quotes. The Application does not download market data and does not transmit any information in order to show it.

To keep the market the same for every player and to prevent clock tampering, the Application reads the date from the HTTP response header of a request to www.apple.com when it opens, or to www.google.com if Apple's server cannot be reached. The request contains no personal information, and the time it returns is kept only on your device. As with any internet request, Apple or Google may see your IP address, subject to their privacy policies.

Market data from the HF Data Library (Elkassabgi 2026), available at hfdatalibrary.com under CC BY 4.0, modified by Vestex (shifted calendar, rescaled and slightly varied price levels, interpolated moves between minutes). Data provided for free by IEX. By accessing or using IEX Historical Data, you agree to the IEX Historical Data Terms of Use.

10. Disclosure to Third Parties

Because the developer receives no personal information, none is sold, rented, licensed or otherwise disclosed to third parties. The developer does not engage in the sale or sharing of personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

11. Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained on your device until you reset your portfolio, which the Application allows every six months, or delete it. Your iCloud backup is kept in your iCloud account until you delete it or use Delete Account & Data. Keychain items may remain after the Application is deleted, so that your portfolio is restored if you reinstall it on the same device.

Settings > Delete Account & Data permanently deletes all data the Application stores on your device, including the portfolio, history, watchlist, alerts, achievements, lesson progress, season and practice data, settings, your saved name and Apple user identifier, widget snapshot, scheduled notifications and all Keychain items (backup, integrity key and clock data), and removes your iCloud backup. It returns the Application to its initial state. Scores already submitted to Game Center remain with Apple and are retained under Apple's policies.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under the General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection in relation to personal information held about you. Because the developer holds no such information, these rights are satisfied by the design of the Application. Requests relating to Game Center, Sign in with Apple or iCloud data should be directed to Apple.

13. Children

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from any person. Because no personal information is collected, the Application does not require verifiable parental consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.

14. Security

Locally stored information is protected by the security measures of your device and operating system. The iCloud backup is sealed (encrypted) before it is stored in your private CloudKit database. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and Apple ID.

15. Changes to This Policy

The developer may amend this Policy from time to time. The date of the most recent revision is stated at the head of this document. Continued use of the Application after a revision takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

Support

Answers to the questions people ask most.

Is any real money involved?

No. Every balance, order and position is simulated. Nothing you do in Vestex can cost or earn real money.

Why didn't my order fill?

The market is open Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM–4:00 PM Eastern. Orders placed while it's closed wait for the next open, and limit and stop orders wait for their price.

Can I start over?

Yes. Settings → Reset Portfolio becomes available six months after your portfolio starts, and every six months after that.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Apple is optional. It asks only for your name and lets Vestex back up your portfolio to your own private iCloud, which the developer cannot read.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete Account & Data. It permanently erases everything stored on your device and your iCloud backup. Scores already sent to Game Center stay with Apple.

Why is my new portfolio missing from the leaderboard?

Each Game Center player has one portfolio on the leaderboard. After Delete Account & Data, a new portfolio's scores are held back until your previous portfolio's six-month reset date.

Are the prices live?

No. Vestex replays real historical market data as if it were happening now. It is a realistic simulation, not a live quote feed, and not investment advice.

How do I contact the developer?

Open a support request at github.com/losthorizzon/vestex-legal/issues. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.
