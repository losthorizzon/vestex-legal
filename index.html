Features

Pro-level tools. Zero real risk.

The tools usually locked behind a premium plan, free from the first trade.

Trade

Any amount, even $5

Fractional shares by dollar amount or whole shares. Market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing stop orders, good for the day or until canceled. Dividend stocks pay cash dividends.

Advanced

Shorting and options

Short selling with collateral rules and margin calls, plus calls and puts priced with Black-Scholes. Lessons unlock them as you go.

Learn

Lessons and a trade coach

Short lessons with quizzes, a glossary, an explanation beside every control, and a coach that reviews your trades.

Compete

One leaderboard, five AI traders

A Game Center leaderboard ranks total return since your portfolio started. Beat five AI traders, earn a skill rating and play in seasons.

Track

Portfolio, widgets, sharing

Performance against the S&P 500, trade history, price alerts, model portfolios, Home and Lock Screen widgets and share cards.

Stay

Streaks and weekend practice

Achievements, daily streaks and a separate practice market on weekends, when the main market is closed.

How it works

Real market mechanics. Simulated money.
